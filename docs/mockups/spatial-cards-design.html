What this shows

This is a flat-page stand-in for the ImmersiveView billboard cards described in docs/dashboard-immersive-mode-design.md. Card positions here are laid out on a 2D grid around a patient placeholder purely so every card is visible at once — the real layout comes from authored points of interest in Immersive.usda, with each card facing the viewer via BillboardComponent.

Basic Information (left) has no S/B/A/R badge, no collapse control, and stays fully visible in Spatial mode, matching the agreed spec. Every other card carries its group badge and collapses to badge + title only. The SBAR rail and mode switch sit in the persistent control window, which is what a real launcher/control window would show while in Spatial mode.

Colors are pulled directly from this project's existing DashboardTheme color assets (dark-appearance values), not invented — DashboardWarning, DashboardCritical, DashboardNormal, DashboardAccent, and the existing glass/stroke tokens. Everything else (card layout, spacing, the SBAR badge shapes) is still open for you to redirect — this is the starting proposal for review, not a locked design.
